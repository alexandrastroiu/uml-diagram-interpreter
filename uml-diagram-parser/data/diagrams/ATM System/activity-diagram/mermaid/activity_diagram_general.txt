flowchart TD
    A([Start]) --> B[Insert ATM Card]
    B --> C[Enter PIN]

    C --> D{PIN Valid?}

    D -- No --> E[Display Error]
    E --> F{Attempts < 3?}
    F -- Yes --> C
    F -- No --> G[Retain Card]
    G --> Z([End])

    D -- Yes --> H[Display Menu]
    H --> I[Select Transaction]

    I --> J{Transaction Type}

    J --> K[Withdraw Cash]
    J --> L[Deposit Funds]
    J --> M[Balance Inquiry]

    K --> N{Sufficient Balance?}
    N -- No --> O[Display Insufficient Funds]
    O --> H
    N -- Yes --> P[Dispense Cash]
    P --> Q[Update Account]

    L --> R[Accept Deposit]
    R --> S[Update Account]

    M --> T[Retrieve Balance]
    T --> U[Display Balance]

    Q --> V{Another Transaction?}
    S --> V
    U --> V

    V -- Yes --> H
    V -- No --> W[Eject Card]
    W --> X[Print Receipt]
    X --> Z([End])
