flowchart TD
    A([Start]) --> B[Member logs into Library Management System]
    B --> C[Search for a book]
    C --> D{Book available?}

    D -- Yes --> E[Select the book]
    E --> F[Submit borrow request]
    F --> G[System verifies member eligibility]

    G --> H{Eligible?}
    H -- Yes --> I[Issue the book]
    I --> J[Update library inventory]
    J --> K[Record borrowing transaction]
    K --> L[Display confirmation]
    L --> M([End])

    H -- No --> N[Display borrowing denied]
    N --> M

    D -- No --> O[Display book unavailable]
    O --> M
