flowchart TD
    A([Start]) --> B[Display Login / Registration Form]
    B --> C[Enter Username and Password]

    C --> D{Authentication Successful?}

    D -- No --> E[Display Authentication Failed Message]
    E --> C

    D -- Yes --> F[Browse Available Books]

    F --> G[View Recommended Books]
    G --> H[Search for a Book]
    H --> I[Apply Filters]
    I --> J[Select Book]
    J --> K[Read Book Details and Reviews]

    K --> L[Request One or Multiple Books]

    L --> M[Wait for System Approval]
    M --> N{Request Approved?}

    N -- No --> F
    N -- Yes --> O[Borrow Book(s)]

    O --> P{Choose Next Action}

    P -->|Return Book| Q{Returned Before Due Date?}
    Q -- Yes --> R[Complete Return]
    Q -- No --> S[Receive Fine]
    S --> R

    P -->|Apply for Renewal| T[Submit Online Renewal Request]
    T --> U{Renewal Approved?}
    U -- Yes --> O
    U -- No --> P

    P -->|Cancel Membership| V{Any Borrowed Books?}
    V -- Yes --> W[Return All Borrowed Books]
    W --> X[Cancel Membership]
    V -- No --> X

    R --> Y{Cancel Membership?}
    Y -- Yes --> V
    Y -- No --> Z([End])

    X --> Z
